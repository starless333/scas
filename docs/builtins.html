Every builtin, in one place.

This inventory is checked against the interpreter registry. Signatures use x for values, expr for symbolic expressions, v for vectors, and A for matrices.

Exact unless approximation is explicitly requested

Derivation variants

Every builtin rendered with => shows evaluated arguments, the mathematical rule applied, and its exact result. The variants below additionally expose specialized internal traces and are selected automatically by ordinary calls in rendered-result position.

det_steps(A)Determinant elimination steps.
rref_steps(A)Row operations.
inverse_steps(A)Augmented-matrix reduction.
matmul_steps(A,B)Product definition.
solve_linear_steps(A,b)Augmented-system reduction.
transpose_steps(A)Index transformation.
trace_steps(A)Diagonal terms and sum.
rank_steps(A)Row reduction and pivot count.
dot_steps(u,v)Component-product sum.
cross_steps(u,v)3D determinant construction.
norm_steps(v)Squared norm then square root.
lu_steps(A)Doolittle decomposition explanation.
qr_steps(A)Modified Gram–Schmidt explanation.
cholesky_steps(A)LLᵀ decomposition explanation.
