Results in text

Read an accessible summary of the computed solution

For A = [[4, 0], [1, 2]], the characteristic polynomial is lambda squared minus 6 lambda plus 8, with eigenvalues 4 and 2. Eigenvectors (2, 1) and (0, 1) form S, while D has diagonal entries 4 and 2. Gauss–Jordan reduction gives inverse(S) = [[1/2, 0], [-1/2, 1]], and S D inverse(S) reconstructs A.

Every diagonalizing matrix independently rescales those two eigenvectors and may swap their order. The same matrices diagonalize inverse(A), whose eigenvalues are 1/4 and 1/2.

For B = [[3/5, 9/10], [2/5, 1/10]], the eigenvalues are 1 and -3/10 with eigenvectors (9, 4) and (1, -1). The transient eigenvalue decays, so the powers converge to P = [[9/13, 9/13], [4/13, 4/13]]. SCAS verifies RREF(P) = [[1, 1], [0, 0]], B P = P, and P squared = P.
